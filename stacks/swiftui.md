# SwiftUI Guidance

- Prefer native SwiftUI controls and interaction behavior where they fit the product.
- Build hierarchy with spacing, typography, materials, and navigation rather than custom decoration everywhere.
- Respect safe areas and Dynamic Type.
- Keep tap targets comfortable.
- Use animation to reinforce state changes, not as constant decoration.
- Test compact screen sizes rather than assuming one iPhone dimension.
- Keep custom materials and glass effects restrained and readable.
- Preserve platform expectations for navigation, sheets, alerts, lists, forms, and destructive actions unless the product has a strong reason not to.
